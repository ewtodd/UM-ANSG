# 73mGe — status and plan (2026-11-25)
<!---->
The energy of the bare 73mGe γ ray: in the pixelated CZT at the OSU reactor (`czt/`, January 2026), and in the Amptek CdTe and Si at PSI's BOA beamline (`cdte/`, `si/`, October 2025). ENSDF gives 68.752 ± 0.007 keV. Ge-detector values (68.79–68.82 keV) include a recoil deposit these detectors do not see, so a result there would mean a bias.

## CZT (`czt/`): closed
<!---->
Closed on 2026-11-25: no further changes or studies. The final steps were the escape fractions measured and applied to every run, the transfer models made consistent and quoted at their midpoint, the escapes' shares within each series taken from Geant4, and Method 2 the direct fit of the no-shield run in place of the rate subtraction. The value sent to the collaborator on 2026-10-27, 68.7567 ± 0.0191 keV, came before that.

Errors and shifts in eV; Ge is the midpoint of the gain and offset transfer models and the transfer model column half their difference; the escape fractions column is the systematic (half the shift between the fractions moved up and down by their uncertainty), the escape correction (with − without the escapes) is shown, not charged; Ge − mean in each run's own total error.

- **Quoted** 68.7548 ± 0.0159 keV, +2.8 eV from ENSDF: the BLUE of the four runs, 68.7548 ± 0.0142 keV (χ²/ndf 0.61; statistics 12.0, calibration offset 3.7 and multiplicative 2.6, systematics: Ge tail 4.6, transfer model 1.1, escape fractions 4.0 eV), with 7.1 eV of outlier removal (the BLUE without Cd 10%, the lowest run) in quadrature (`results/czt_ge.result`). Method 1 (68.7489 ± 0.0165) and Method 2 (68.7706 ± 0.0264 keV) agree to 22 ± 31 eV, the shared escape fractions included.

- **Selection and pixel gains.** Single-interaction events within 2 mm of the cathode (z ≥ 8 mm) on a pixel centre, without pile-up (under 3 µs of live time and in the same millisecond as the previous event), each energy times its pixel's gain: 558.456 keV over its 558 keV centroid in the 01/16 graphite-castle run.
- **Day pixel map.** Near 70–96 keV the 558 keV gains leave the crystals 80–300 eV apart and the pixels 110 eV rms. Every event is aligned on its day's map (`czt/PixelAlignment.hpp`): each pixel's line position against its run's summed spectrum, averaged over the day's beam runs, on Pb Kα on 01/13 and on the 95.902 keV line on 01/15, where Pb Kα barely stands above the continuum. A pixel measured to 100 eV or better at 68.75 keV gets the factor μ_run/μ_pixel; a day's source runs take its map.
- **Method 1, 01/13, in-situ.** Each background run (Pb Kα2/Kα1) is fitted together with its signal run (the same lines plus Ge) over 65–80 keV in an unbinned likelihood, first in raw energy, with one Pb shape and one low tail (a Gaussian with an exponential tail, τ/σ ≤ 20) on a flat continuum. Cu 10% is calibrated with a pol1 through Am-241 and its background run's Pb Kα, the reference. Each signal run, Cu included, takes it through its own Pb Kα, as a gain (the raw axis scaled about the reference's intercept) or as an offset (`TRANSFER=offset`), and each Cd background run through its own; any mix of the two lies between them, so each run is quoted at their midpoint.
- **Method 2, 01/15, direct fit of the no-shield run.** A pol1 through Am-241 and Ba-133 80.998 keV from the source runs. The signal run alone, its background run not subtracted, in an unbinned likelihood over 62–78 keV: Ge on a linear continuum (not flat here), its low tail held at the source lines' (Am-241 and Ba-133 80.998 keV interpolated to Ge: 0.071 of the Gaussian's height, τ/σ 9.6), with the 95.902 keV line's escapes, which a direct fit once lacked, as fixed lines of Ge's shape. The fit starts cold, without a saved seed. Systematic: Ge's tail free instead (16.9 eV; it fits smaller and shorter, a full-range ratio 0.21 at τ/σ 3.4 against 0.54 at 9.6). χ²/ndf 3.2 on comb-period bins, with runs of same-sign pulls near 66 and at 73–75 keV that no source in the setup explains; the fit error is not scaled.
- **Both methods** carry the 95.902 keV line's Cd and Te K X-ray escapes as fixed lines, at the fractions `czt/CaptureEscapeFraction.cpp` measures in the 01/15 beam background run (no shield, no Pb): an unbinned fit of both Cd escape pairs as one yield, the series tied by Geant4's ratios and each series' lines by its shares, gives Cd Kβ 0.0140 ± 0.0018 (12.5%; the set 0.0515, 0.0140, 0.0028, 0.0008 of the 95.902 keV line's Gaussian yield). Every run takes them: the line's position in the crystal shows no dependence on the Cd shield beyond the variation between unshielded runs, and the Cd-shield runs' own line is too weak to tell.
- **Combination** (`CombineGeResult`): a BLUE with correlated blocks for the Am reference (the 01/13 rows), the gain transfer, the transfer model (half of offset − gain, each run at the midpoint) and the escape fractions (half of up − down, every run); fits, anchors and the direct fit's tail systematic on the diagonal; the outlier removal on top.
- **01/14 and 01/16 are not used**: 01/14's calibration cannot be carried over from 01/13, and the 01/16 graphite castle differs from the other runs in too many ways.

## PSI CdTe (`cdte/`)
<!---->
The CdTe (DT5780 channel 1) on `PulseHeightProcessor` energies, in rise-time slices because slow pulses lose charge to hole trapping. Each slice is fitted over 62–89 keV with every line at its true energy on a free scale (Pb Kα2/Kα1, the Pb Kβ groups, the 95.902 keV line's Cd and Te escapes, Ge free), one EMG with a linear width and a linear continuum. The result is the weighted mean of the five slices up to 320 ns, on the five Ge beam runs (10/09–10/11).
<!---->
| Term | eV | |
|---|---|---|
| statistics | 16.4 | weighted mean of five slices, 6.6 eV, × √(χ²/ndf) 2.47 of the slices ≤ 300 ns (18.4/3) |
| outlier removal | 10.7 | largest shift without the highest (0–270 ns), the lowest (290–300 ns), or both slices |
| lineshape | 5.5 | Ge's tail fitted on its own |
| escapes | 4.5 | Te Kα/Cd Kβ ± its error and per slice; Cd Kβ/Kα 0.324 against Geant4's 0.275 |
| Pb Kβ blend | 1.4 | Kβ3/Kβ1 (and K-N2/K-N3) ± 10%; Kβ5, K-N4,5, K-O2,3 ± 20% |
| extrapolation | 22.8 | the scale's curvature free (Ge lies below the lines that fix the scale) |
| slice range | 0.8 | slices ≤ 300 ns instead (statistical spread 1.1 eV) |
| slice combination | 4.7 | a random-effects mean of the slices (between-slice spread 32.7 eV) |
<!---->
- **Extrapolation** is the full shift with the curvature free. It is a noisy statistic (20.6 ± 9.5 eV over replicas of the same data), mostly the 0–270 and 300–320 ns slices, where the curvature is nearly degenerate with the escapes; the curvature's quadrature cost instead gives ± 0.0249 keV (Plan 5).
- **10/10 carries most of the slices' disagreement**: without its two Ge-target files (a scratch rerun) the slices ≤ 300 ns agree at χ² 9.0/3 instead of 18.4/3 and Ge reads 68.7537 ± 0.0283 keV. 10/10 stays in.
- **The move to `PulseHeightProcessor`** (+5.3 eV, from 68.7448) is reprocessing scatter, not a correction (`cdte/ReprocessingComparison.cpp`). The old `trap/` trees are kept until that comparison is no longer needed.

- **Records whose baseline sits at the bottom of the ADC range** are dropped.

## CZT findings, recorded and not pursued
<!---->
- **Calibration curvature.** 01/13: the in-run Pb Kα1 → Kβ1 slope over 75–85 keV (0.98817 ± 0.00052) matches Am → Kα (0.98822), so a quadratic of that size moves Ge by about 1 eV. 01/15: Ba-133 53.16 keV reads −5.6 ± 16.7 eV on the linear calibration; above 81 keV the response is strongly nonlinear.
- **Run-to-run shifts.** The scale moves between runs by tens of eV, up to 130 eV at the 01/13 Cd → Cu shield change. No count-rate model holds across days.
- **Depth.** Lines read higher deeper in the crystal (Am 220 eV, Pb Kα1 130 eV across four depth slices); a per-depth calibration left Method 1 near 68.763 keV in throwaway fits, before the pixel map.
- **The energy comb** does not affect the unbinned in-situ fits: dithering every energy by ±132 eV moves the 01/13 rows by −8 to +4 eV and Method 1 by about −3 eV.
- **Testbed** (`czt/InjectionTest.cpp`, `czt/SummarizeInjection.cpp`). A line injected into a synthetic replica of each pair is recovered: Cu +1.8 ± 2.7 eV (50 trials), Cd 10% −11.4 ± 8.3, Cd 25% −4.3 ± 9.2 eV. On halves of the real background runs, rate-sub closes (−2.1 ± 2.7 eV together), while in-situ reads +12 to +33 eV. Halves of eight synthetic background runs, each held fixed through its trials, scatter by 16.0 (Cu), 21.1 (Cd 10%) and 9.1 eV (Cd 25%) from one background run to the next, so the real runs' offsets are that run's fluctuation, not a real-background effect. These numbers are from 10/23; a 3-trial recheck on the current code (2026-10-27, `plots/testbed/injection_recheck_*`) agrees for the synthetic replicas and the rate-sub halves. The testbed still runs the rate subtraction, not the direct fit that is now Method 2.

## Plan
<!---->
1. **The CdTe extrapolation term** (dissertation only: the letter cites the collaborator's preprint, arXiv:2609.20848, for the PSI values, and our CdTe and Si reprocessing goes only in the dissertation): the full curvature-free shift or the curvature's quadrature cost, and whether to move the slice edges off the rise estimator's sawtooth peaks (280, 300, 320 ns).
2. **Recommended value** for the paper: an error-weighted average of the CZT (this work), the collaborator's CdTe and Si, and Jones and Kraner (1971, 1975), as in the collaborator's paper (adopted 68.7536 ± 0.0034 keV there); our CdTe and Si (the collaborator's six Si groups as they are) are cross-checks of the same data. Only measurements without a Ge detector enter: neutrons cannot be kept out of a detector (the CZT sees ¹¹³Cd(n,γ) from its own crystal in every configuration, behind the Cd foil too), and captures on a Ge detector's own ⁷²Ge add the recoil to the 68.75 keV line. That leaves out Collar et al. (with it the value moves −0.6 eV) and any Jones and Kraner value taken with a Ge(Li) detector. Their 1971 paper (Phys. Rev. C 4, 125) lists seven energies, (p,p′γ) ×4, (n,γ), (α,α′γ) ×2, from "both Ge(Li) and Si(Li) detectors" without saying which; only a (p,p′) spectrum is shown as Si(Li). The 1975 value (Phys. Rev. A 11, 1347), 68.755 ± 0.005 keV, is ⁷²Ge(n,γ) on a GeO₂ sample seen with a Si(Li). Both papers calibrate on Am 59.537 keV and Bearden's Pb Kα; on DDEP and SRD 128 their energies rise by +2.015 ± 0.001 eV: their published energies are positions on an arbitrary scale, and the calibration is rebuilt on today's values as a straight line through all three lines, equally weighted (+2.015 eV) or weighted by the stated uncertainties of the old and the new energies on both axes (+2.016 eV; old Am 1 eV, Bearden's Pb probable errors about 0.9 eV, new 0.1-0.24 eV); a quadratic through three points is not used (`evaluation/RecommendedValue.cpp`, `plots/evaluation`). With the 1971 (p,p′γ) group (its spectrum shown taken with a Si(Li)) and the 1975 value, Jones and Kraner recalibrated: **68.7546 ± 0.0037 keV** (χ²/ndf 0.8/5; 68.7531 unadjusted). The PSI values are not adjusted (user, 2026-11-24): taken to be calibrated like the collaborator's 2024 paper (Phys. Rev. D 110, 083043: DDEP Am, Bearden Pb Kα; the 2026 preprint does not say), today's Pb Kα would raise them by 0.62 eV with equal weights and at most 0.85 eV (lines with Am weighted at least as each Pb line: 0.42–0.85 eV), far below their uncertainties. The 2024 summed peak, 68.8158 keV on the same energies, is likewise not adjusted: ionization 61.2 ± 5.9 eV, quenching factor 24.0 ± 2.3 % (254.1 eV recoil). The Si inputs are the draft's results table (Si-1 68.7529 ± 0.0114, Si-2 68.7555 ± 0.0128 keV; its per-group values average to 68.7543); nothing more is asked of the collaborator (2026-11-19, deadlines: dissertation 12/01, papers 12/15). Open: correlations among the Jones and Kraner values (J&K 1975 carries 56% of the weight), treated as independent for now.
3. **Write-up**: a PRC letter (the measurement, the CZT fit, the non-Ge recommended value), drafted 2026-11-19 in `~/Papers/Writing/WIP-73mGe-PRC` (`experiment.tex`, `results.tex`, `recommended.tex`, figures from `czt/FitFigure.cpp` and `evaluation/RecommendedValue.cpp`).
